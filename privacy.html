Privacy Policy

SwissPeak Partners GmbH · Last updated: August 2026

SwissPeak Partners GmbH takes the protection of personal data seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and process personal data when you visit our website, contact us, use our services, or apply for a position through our website or connected recruiting systems.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to visitors of our website, business contacts, clients, candidates and other individuals whose personal data we process in connection with our business activities, our website, our communication channels and our recruitment processes.

Data We Process

We process personal data that you provide to us directly, data generated through your use of our website, and data we receive in connection with recruitment and business communications. Depending on the context, this may include your name, e-mail address, telephone number, company details, the content of your messages, your CV, employment history, qualifications, salary expectations, work authorisation information, interview notes, and other correspondence and information you submit in a contact request or application.

Contact Requests

When you contact us through a contact form, by e-mail, by telephone or through other channels, we process the information you provide in order to respond to your request, communicate with you and, where relevant, prepare or perform our services.

Applications and Recruitment

When you apply for a position advertised by or through SwissPeak Partners, we process your application data in order to assess your suitability for a role, conduct the recruitment process, communicate with you, arrange interviews, evaluate profiles and, where relevant, present suitable candidates to hiring companies in the context of a client mandate. Access to applicant data is limited to the authorised members of our team involved in the relevant process and, where applicable, the client responsible for the vacancy. We process only the data required for the application and placement process.

Applicant Tracking System (Recruitee)

Job advertisements and the online application function on our website are operated through the applicant tracking system Recruitee. When you apply through our website, the information and documents you submit, including your CV, are captured and stored in Recruitee and made available to the authorised members of our team involved in the relevant recruitment process. Retention periods for candidate records are configured in the system and determine how long applications, documents and process-related notes are kept. Recruitee acts as our processor for this purpose.

LinkedIn

Our website contains links to our profiles on LinkedIn, and job advertisements may be published on or shared through LinkedIn. If you interact with our LinkedIn presence or apply through LinkedIn, LinkedIn processes your data under its own terms and privacy notice, which apply in addition to this Privacy Policy. Where an application reaches us through LinkedIn, we then process the data within our recruitment process as described above.

Appointment Scheduling

Where we offer the option to book a call or meeting online, this may be provided through a third-party scheduling service such as Calendly. If you book an appointment through such a service, the details you enter, for example your name, e-mail address and any information you provide about your request, are processed in order to arrange and confirm the appointment. The scheduling provider's own privacy terms may apply in addition to this Privacy Policy.

Payments

Where we offer paid services, for example career consulting packages, payments are processed through the payment service provider Stripe, typically via a Stripe payment link or checkout page. When you make a payment, the data required to process it, such as your name, e-mail address and payment details, is provided to and processed by Stripe under its own terms and privacy notice. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. We receive confirmation of payment and the information needed for our records.

Purposes of Processing

We process personal data in order to respond to enquiries, communicate with clients, candidates and business contacts, provide recruitment and HR consulting services, conduct recruitment and placement processes, assess candidate suitability, manage applications, operate and secure our website, analyse website usage in aggregated form, comply with legal obligations, and assert or defend legal claims.

Legal Basis

We process personal data on an applicable legal basis, in particular where processing is necessary for pre-contractual steps, the performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, our legitimate business interests, or on the basis of your consent where consent is required. In the recruitment context, candidate data is processed because it is necessary to assess a potential employment relationship or placement.

Disclosure to Third Parties

We may share personal data with carefully selected service providers and processors who support our business operations, for example website hosting and platform providers, form and communication providers, recruiting software providers, payment service providers such as Stripe, IT providers and administrative service providers. In the context of a recruitment mandate, candidate data may also be shared with the relevant client or hiring company where this is necessary for the application or placement process. We ensure that only authorised persons receive access to applicant data.

Retention

We store personal data only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, for as long as required by law, or for as long as needed to protect legitimate interests such as documentation, dispute resolution or compliance. In our applicant tracking system, candidate retention periods are configured and determine how long candidate details, application materials and interview records are stored. If an application does not lead to employment or placement, application data is generally retained only for as long as necessary to complete the recruitment process and to meet legal obligations, or for a longer period where you have consented to being considered for future opportunities. Candidate records can subsequently be deleted or anonymised.

Website Technical Data

When you visit our website, technical data may be processed automatically, such as IP-related connection data, browser type, device information, operating system, referring pages, pages visited, and the date and time of access, together with other server log data required for the operation, security and troubleshooting of the website.

Website Platform and Forms

Our website is built and hosted using the website platform Dorik. Data you enter on the website, for example through a contact form, is processed within this environment as part of providing the website. The provider acts as our processor for the operation of the website.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, cookieless web analytics to measure website usage in aggregated form. These analytics are designed not to track visitors across websites and not to collect data that identifies you personally. Where Plausible Analytics is used, it operates on a cookieless basis and processes only aggregated usage data.

Cookies

We use only cookies and similar technologies that are technically necessary to operate and secure the website, or that are activated through optional third-party services you choose to use. We do not use non-essential tracking cookies. If tools such as embedded media, maps, LinkedIn features, advertising tags or additional external scripts are added in future, we will update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

International Transfers

Personal data may be processed in Switzerland, in the European Economic Area or in other countries, depending on the service providers used. Where personal data is transferred abroad, we implement appropriate safeguards where required by applicable law.

Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure.

Your Rights

Under applicable Swiss data protection law and, where applicable, other data protection laws, you may request information about your personal data, request the correction of inaccurate data, request deletion, restrict certain processing, object to certain processing, and withdraw your consent with effect for the future where processing is based on consent.

Exercising Your Rights

If you wish to exercise your rights or have questions about the processing of your personal data, please contact us at hello@swisspeak-partners.ch. In order to process such requests, we may need to verify your identity.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if our website, recruitment processes, service providers, legal obligations or business activities change. The version published on our website at the relevant time applies.
