FAQ

How small can we start?

+

One role. There's no minimum team size to get started.

Who leads the team?

+

You do, unless you want us to. Either way, the team works to your priorities.

How fast can we be up and running?

+

Depending on scope: from a couple of days for a single hire, to four to eight weeks for more complex engagements, brief to first working day.

What if someone doesn't fit?

+

We replace them at no extra cost, covered by our replacement guarantee.

Can we own the entity instead, or switch later?

+

Yes to both. We can build the entity and hand it over, and contracts migrate later without the team noticing.

Can you place outside Europe?

+

On request. We'll say upfront if it's a bad idea for the role in question.
