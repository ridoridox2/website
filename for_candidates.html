Career Consulting

Need more than recruitment?
We also offer personal career support.

Our consulting services help you stand out in Switzerland's competitive job market. No matter if you are exploring new opportunities, planning a transition or preparing for a leadership role: we combine deep market insights, with practical knowledge, CV optimisation, salary benchmarking, interview preparation and career strategy. This way, you always walk away with clarity, confidence and a clear next step.

Swiss market insight

In-depth knowledge of the Swiss job market, industry trends, and what employers actually expect from candidates.

Personalized guidance

Advice and application documents tailored to your specific skills, experience, and goals, not generic templates.

Practical, actionable support

CV optimization, salary benchmarking, interview preparation, and skills assessment you can put to use immediately.

Good to know

Career consulting isn't recruitment. And it never influences it.

These are completely independent services. Purchasing a consulting package has zero impact on how we evaluate you as a recruitment candidate. There's no preferential treatment for placement, our consulting and recruitment services run side by side without ever influencing one another.
